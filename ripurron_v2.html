🌐 Online Multiplayer
Peer-to-peer · no accounts · no servers · totally free
HOW IT WORKS — Your browser connects directly to your friend's browser using WebRTC — the same technology behind Google Meet and Discord video calls. No signup, no server, no lag from a middleman. You just need to share a 4-letter room code.
1
Enter your rider name
Type your name in the box on the main screen — this is what friends will see floating above your bike. Saved automatically so you only need to do it once.
2
Click RIDE NOW, then open Multiplayer
From the ☰ menu click 🌐 Online Multiplayer. Your unique room code appears instantly — something like K7RX. Click it to copy.
3
Share your code with a friend
Send the code over iMessage, Discord, Snapchat, anywhere — just 4 letters. Your friend opens the same game link and enters your code in the JOIN box. Connection is instant — you'll both see each other's bikes appear in-game.
4
Ride together
Friends appear as blue-tinted bikes with their name and speed floating above. You can both do tricks, race, hit ramps — whatever. Switch maps and they'll see you teleport (they stay on their map unless they switch too). The 🟢 ONLINE badge at the top shows connection status.
